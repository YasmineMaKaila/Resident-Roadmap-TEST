# Notice — internal Cortland material

This repository contains **Cortland brand and operational content**: the Resident
Roadmap artwork, the Cortland logo, the colour palette, and the leasing and
screening policy copy shown in the nine information cards.

It is published here for internal use and review. It is **not** open-source
content and carries no licence granting reuse, redistribution or modification by
anyone outside Cortland.

- Treat this repository as **private** unless someone with authority over the
  Cortland brand and over the leasing/screening policy copy has approved making
  it public. Publishing it with GitHub Pages puts the policy content on a public
  URL.
- The original `The_Resident_Roadmap_Final_July2026.pdf` is intentionally **not
  committed** (see `.gitignore`). The build does not need it; the extracted page
  artwork under `src/assets/pages/` is enough.
- The information-card copy is reproduced verbatim from that PDF, including its
  errors. Before this is used for training, read
  [`docs/SOURCE-CONTENT-NOTES.md`](docs/SOURCE-CONTENT-NOTES.md) — it lists a
  visible misspelling, a subject/verb disagreement, two footnote markers with no
  footnote, and one point that may warrant a legal check.
- **TT Norms Pro** is a licensed typeface and is *not* embedded as a webfont.
  The roadmap artwork carries it as vector outlines (converted from the PDF);
  the surrounding interface uses TT Norms Pro only if the viewer already has it
  installed, and otherwise falls back to a similar sans-serif.
